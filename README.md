# NoeticAI

A private AI for your Mac. Noetic 1.0 chats, reasons, searches the web, and writes and runs code for you, entirely on your Mac. Nothing you type is sent anywhere except the web searches it makes for you.

The name comes from the Greek *noētikos* (νοητικός), "of the mind".

## Download

1. Download **NoeticAI.zip** from the [latest release](https://github.com/SashaIsBetter/NoeticAI-releases/releases/latest).
2. Double-click the zip, then drag **NoeticAI** into your **Applications** folder.
3. Open it. The first time, macOS says it can't verify the developer (the app isn't notarized by Apple):
   - Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway** next to NoeticAI.
   - Or, in Terminal: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/NoeticAI.app`
4. Noetic downloads its brain once (about 6 GB). After that it works offline.

**Coming from Turing AI?** Install NoeticAI the same way. Your chats, memories and settings move over the first time you open it, and the old app goes to the Trash.

## Requirements

- A Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer), 16 GB of memory recommended
- macOS 15 or later
- About 8 GB of free disk space

## Updates

NoeticAI updates itself. It checks this page every few hours, downloads new versions in the background, and installs them when you click **Update ready · Restart** in the sidebar, or the next time you quit it. Keep it in your Applications folder so it can update.
